# Local Business Connect & ROI Module Guide

## Consent & Privacy Principles
- Retailers explicitly consent (`consentGiven = true`) before their listing is submitted for Admin verification.
- Public Visitors only see `verified` listings; `pending` and `rejected` listings remain restricted to Admin/Retailer review workflows.
- Household-level water records do not exist in the portal and are never used to target consumers.

## ROI Estimator Formulas (Strictly Labelled as Estimates)
- `Estimated Additional Revenue = Additional Attributable Enquiries × (Conversion Rate / 100) × Average Order Value`
- `Estimated Gross Profit = Estimated Additional Revenue × (Gross Margin / 100)`
- `Estimated Incremental Net Benefit = Estimated Gross Profit − Additional Business Costs`
